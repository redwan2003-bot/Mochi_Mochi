Circuit Safety & Protection Guarantees

  • ✓ Physical SPDT Battery Isolation Switch: Series cut-off on LiPo BAT+ prevents quiescent drain in storage and provides true zero-power cold isolation when flashing USB firmware.
  • ✓ 1N4148 Diode Reverse Blocking: Protects ESP32-S3 GPIO7 from seeing the raw 5V charger rail when the TP4056 red LED is off.
  • ✓ 10kΩ/20kΩ Voltage Divider: Accurately steps the 5.0V dock line down to 3.33V for safe logic level on GPIO5.
  • ✓ Protected 1S LiPo Cell: Integrated PCM protection board protects against overcharge (4.2V), overdischarge (2.8V), and short circuits.
  • ✓ Half-Duplex I²S Bus Arbitration: Firmware gates microphone RX and speaker TX sequentially to prevent speaker feedback.
  • ✓ USB Flashing Interlock: Flash firmware via Native USB CDC only when undocked and battery disconnected.
